Title: Egyptian Pound Stabilizes Near EGP 48.5 to the Dollar

The Egyptian pound traded relatively stable this week, hovering around EGP 48.5 per US dollar on the official interbank market, according to Central Bank of Egypt data. This follows several months of gradual depreciation earlier in the year.

Economists note that improved foreign currency inflows from tourism revenue and remittances have eased pressure on the currency. The Central Bank's foreign reserves rose to $47.2 billion last month, up from $45.8 billion three months earlier.

Market participants are watching closely for the Central Bank's upcoming monetary policy meeting, with most analysts expecting rates to remain unchanged at 27.25%.
