Title: EGX30 Closes Higher as Banking Stocks Lead Gains

Cairo — The EGX30 index closed up 1.8% on Wednesday, driven primarily by gains in the banking sector. Commercial International Bank (CIB) rose 3.2%, while QNB Alahli gained 2.1%. Trading volume reached EGP 1.4 billion, roughly 15% above the 30-day average.

Analysts attributed the rally to expectations that the Central Bank of Egypt will hold interest rates steady at its next meeting, reducing pressure on bank margins. The real estate sector lagged, with Talaat Moustafa Group down 0.9% amid concerns over rising construction costs.

Foreign investors were net buyers for the third consecutive session, purchasing a net EGP 210 million in Egyptian equities.
