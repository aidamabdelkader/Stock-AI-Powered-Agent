Title: Telecom Egypt Announces Special Dividend After Asset Sale

Telecom Egypt's board approved a special cash dividend of EGP 3.75 per share, funded by proceeds from the recent sale of a minority stake in its data center subsidiary. The total dividend payout amounts to approximately EGP 3.4 billion.

The record date for the dividend is set for the 15th of next month, with payment to follow within two weeks. This is in addition to the company's regular annual dividend, which was already distributed earlier this year.

Telecom Egypt shares gained 6.1% on the announcement, though some analysts cautioned that one-off dividends funded by asset sales should not be read as a signal of sustainably higher payouts going forward.
