Title: Analysts Split on Banking Sector Outlook for Next Quarter

Investment banks remain divided on the outlook for Egyptian banking stocks heading into the next quarter. Naeem Brokerage raised its rating on CIB to "Overweight," citing strong net interest margins and improving asset quality, with a price target of EGP 92 per share.

In contrast, EFG Hermes maintained a "Neutral" rating on the sector, warning that a potential interest rate cut later in the year could compress bank margins even as loan growth accelerates. EFG Hermes set a more conservative price target of EGP 78 for CIB.

Both firms agree that asset quality has improved, with non-performing loan ratios across major banks falling to their lowest levels in three years. The disagreement centers primarily on interest rate trajectory and its impact on net interest income.
