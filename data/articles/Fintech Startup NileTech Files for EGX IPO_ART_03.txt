Title: Fintech Startup NileTech Files for EGX IPO, Targets EGP 800 Million

NileTech, a Cairo-based payments and digital lending startup, has filed for an initial public offering on the Egyptian Exchange, aiming to raise approximately EGP 800 million. The company plans to offer 25% of its shares to the public, valuing NileTech at roughly EGP 3.2 billion.

Founded in 2019, NileTech processes over EGP 12 billion in annual payment volume and has expanded into consumer microloans over the past two years. The IPO proceeds are earmarked for expansion into Saudi Arabia and the UAE, as well as technology infrastructure upgrades.

The offering is expected to open for subscription within six weeks, pending regulatory approval from the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA).
