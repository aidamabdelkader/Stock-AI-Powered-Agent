Title: FRA Introduces New Disclosure Rules for AI-Based Investment Tools

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) issued new draft guidelines requiring any platform offering AI-generated investment recommendations to clearly disclose that such outputs do not constitute licensed financial advice, unless the provider holds an appropriate investment advisory license.

The draft rules also require platforms to maintain audit logs of AI-generated recommendations for a minimum of five years, and to implement human review processes for any recommendation that could materially affect a retail investor's portfolio.

The FRA opened a 45-day public comment period before the rules take effect. Industry participants, including several fintech platforms, are expected to submit feedback on implementation timelines and technical requirements.
