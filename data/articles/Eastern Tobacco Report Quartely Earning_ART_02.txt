Title: Eastern Tobacco Reports 22% Jump in Quarterly Profit

Eastern Company (Eastern Tobacco) reported net profit of EGP 1.35 billion for the third quarter, up 22% year-over-year, beating analyst estimates of EGP 1.1 billion. Revenue grew 18% to EGP 4.2 billion, driven by higher domestic cigarette prices and stable export volumes to Gulf markets.

The company's board approved an interim dividend of EGP 2.50 per share, payable next month. Management cited cost-control measures and a favorable currency environment as key drivers of margin expansion, with gross margin improving to 34% from 29% a year earlier.

Eastern Tobacco shares rose 4.5% following the announcement, closing at EGP 18.90.
