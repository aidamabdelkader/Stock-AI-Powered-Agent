Title: Real Estate Stocks Under Pressure as Construction Costs Rise

Egyptian real estate developers are facing margin compression as steel and cement prices climbed 12% and 8% respectively over the past quarter. Talaat Moustafa Group, Palm Hills Developments, and Emaar Misr have all seen their stock prices decline between 3% and 7% over the past month.

Despite the cost pressures, unit sales have remained resilient, with several developers reporting record pre-sales driven by installment-based payment plans that shield buyers from inflation. Palm Hills reported EGP 9.6 billion in new sales contracts for the quarter, up 14% year-over-year.

Analysts remain divided: some see the sector as oversold given strong demand fundamentals, while others warn that margin pressure could persist into next year if input costs stay elevated.
